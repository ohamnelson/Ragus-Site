Privacy Policy

What Ragus keeps, where it goes, and how to delete all of it.

Last updated 13 September 2026

Ragus lets you record what you eat by describing it in your own words. To do that it has to store what you tell it, and send your description to an AI service that turns it into nutrition figures. This page explains exactly what is kept, where it goes, and how to delete all of it.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an account identifier and, usually, your email address. We never see your password. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address Apple gives us.
  • Your profile. Sex, year of birth, height, activity level and time zone. These are used to calculate your resting energy expenditure and daily target, and nothing else.
  • Your body measurements. Weight, and body-fat percentage if you enter it, with the date recorded.
  • Your goal. Target weight and the pace you chose.
  • What you log. The exact text you type or speak, and the food and activity items derived from it, including calories and macronutrients.
  • Your step count, only if you connect Apple Health. Ragus asks Health for read access to your daily step total and nothing else — not workouts, heart rate, or any other category. Each day's total is stored so walking can be counted towards that day. Nothing is ever written to Health. You can disconnect at any time from the You tab, and the app returns to using the activity level you chose.
  • A notification address, only if you allow notifications. A token that identifies this installation of the app on your phone, so a reminder can reach it. It contains no personal information and is removed if you delete the app or your account.

That is the whole list. There are no advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking software of any kind in the app, and no location data.

Health data

Data read from Apple Health is used for one purpose: calculating how much energy you burned walking on a given day. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or analytics, never sold, and never shared with anyone except the services below that store and run the app. It is deleted with everything else when you delete your account.

Voice

If you log by speaking, your speech is transcribed by Apple's speech recognition built into iOS, which may process the audio on Apple's servers. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Ragus receives only the resulting text; we never store or transmit audio recordings.

Who else sees your data

  • Anthropic. The text of what you logged is sent to the Claude API to be turned into structured nutrition data. It is sent for that purpose alone. Your name, email and account identifier are not included.
  • Supabase. Provides the authentication and the database where your account and logs are stored.
  • FastAPI Cloud. Runs the server the app talks to.
  • Expo. Relays push notifications to your phone, if you allow them. Expo receives the notification address and the text of the reminder, nothing else.
  • Resend. Sends the one email Ragus sends — a welcome when you finish setting up. Resend receives your email address for that delivery.
  • Apple and Google. Only as sign-in providers, and only at the moment you choose to sign in.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone for any purpose beyond running the app.

How it is protected

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with TLS. Every request must carry a valid sign-in token, and every database query is filtered to your own account, so one person's data cannot be returned to another.

Deleting everything

Open the You tab and choose Delete account. This permanently removes every meal and activity you have logged, all of your weigh-ins, your goal, your profile, any step totals read from Apple Health, the notification address for your phone, and the sign-in itself. It cannot be undone and nothing is retained afterwards.

Text you previously logged will already have been processed by Anthropic; their retention of API data is governed by their own policies.

Children

Ragus is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who receives it, the date at the top of this page will change and the new version will be published here.
