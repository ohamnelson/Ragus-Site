Support

Help with Ragus, and the questions that come up most.

Something broken, something wrong, or something you want changed — write to dev@ragus.io and you'll get a reply.

Common questions

  • The calories look wrong. Every item shows the portion Ragus assumed. Portion size is the largest source of error, which is why the assumption is always stated rather than hidden.
  • Where does my daily target come from? The You tab shows the full calculation: resting burn, your activity multiplier, what you burn in a day, and the deficit for your chosen pace. Nothing is hidden behind a single number.
  • It didn't understand what I typed. Ragus only reads descriptions of food eaten and activity done. Anything else is declined on purpose.
  • How do I delete my account? The You tab, then Delete account. It removes everything, permanently.
